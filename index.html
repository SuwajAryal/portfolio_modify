    PLACE & EDUCATION

    A perspective shaped by Nepal.

    Himalayan headwaters and river systems are the landscape behind my work in water resources and hydropower.

    A changing climate flows downstream.

    Nepal is among the countries most vulnerable to climate change. Retreating glaciers, intense rainfall, and changing river flows connect risks across the Himalayas, hills, and Terai. Erosion and landslides upstream can carry consequences downstream, where floods threaten farms, infrastructure, and communities.

    Understanding these connected river systems is one reason I want to work on reliable models for water and sediment.

    Climate context: World Bank Group, Nepal CCDR (2022) ↗
