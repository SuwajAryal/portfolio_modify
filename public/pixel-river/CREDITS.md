# Pixel river artwork

This Nepal-inspired composition arranges existing community pixel artwork.
The scene layout and river channels are illustrative, not geographic data.
Source PNGs are included unmodified. Canvas display crops and scales them using
nearest-neighbour sampling, composes the terrain and river banks, and rotates
the existing water animation downward. No AI-generated artwork is used.

CC0 per the OpenGameArt listing; supplied notice included in ansimuz-license.txt.

## Flowing water

Animated flowing water 2d 16x16 — InThePixel.
https://opengameart.org/content/animated-flowing-water-2d-16x16
CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Original: water_00_strip13.png, renamed flowing-water.png.
The artist's 13 animation frames are cropped, rotated 90 degrees counterclockwise,
and tiled inside the channels. The original right-to-left motion becomes downhill
motion. There is no claimed flow speed or representation of a specific river.
