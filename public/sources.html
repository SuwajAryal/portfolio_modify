The scroll-driven education journey combines community mapping software and real geographic data. It uses no AI-generated imagery or hand-drawn terrain.

A map that follows the story

MapLibre GL JS renders the globe, street maps, and camera transitions. Its official scroll-to-location example guided the chapter-based integration. MapLibre is BSD-3-Clause licensed.

Map style and geographic data

OpenFreeMap provides the existing Liberty map style and hosted tiles, using OpenMapTiles and data from OpenStreetMap contributors. Their attribution is preserved on the map.

Made with Natural Earth for the optional Nepal outline, from its public-domain 1:110m country dataset.

The campus marker is an approximate location within the Pulchowk Campus area. Both degrees share that campus; no degree-specific building or surveyed position is implied.

Motion, icons, and type

  • Motion — restrained section transitions. MIT.
  • Lucide — interface icons. ISC, including its Feather-derived notices.
  • Inter by Rasmus Andersson — typography, served through Google Fonts. SIL Open Font License.
