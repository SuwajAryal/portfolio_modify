The people and projects
behind this experience.

The Earth explorer combines community software and real geographic data. It uses no AI-generated imagery or hand-drawn terrain.

Interactive Earth

React Globe.gl by Vasco Asturiano provides the globe, controls, and geographic layers. Its official country-layer example guided the integration. It uses Three.js to render the scene. These projects are MIT-licensed.

Countries and rivers

Made with Natural Earth. Country boundaries are from the 1:110m dataset; river centre lines are from the 1:50m dataset. Natural Earth releases its map data into the public domain.

These are generalised map features, suitable for exploring the world. They are not a river-flow simulation, survey, or comprehensive map of Nepal’s smaller rivers.

Motion, icons, and type

  • Motion — restrained section transitions. MIT.
  • Lucide — interface icons. ISC, including its Feather-derived notices.
  • Inter by Rasmus Andersson — typography, served through Google Fonts. SIL Open Font License.
